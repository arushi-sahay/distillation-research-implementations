# Distillation Research Implementations

An open collection of independent implementations of
influential research papers in knowledge distillation.

## About

Knowledge distillation encompasses a diverse set of
techniques for transferring knowledge between machine
learning models.

Over the years, researchers have explored different
approaches to knowledge transfer across domains,
architectures and learning objectives. Consequently,
distillation does not follow one universal formulation
or implementation strategy.

This repository aims to explore these developments
through the implementation of influential research papers.

Each paper is approached independently, with its
original problem formulation, methodology and
experimental context serving as the foundation
for its implementation.

The goal is to make research understandable through
code, encourage reproducibility, and provide a
collaborative resource for students, researchers
and developers interested in knowledge distillation.

## Guiding Principles

### 1. Fidelity to Original Research

Every implementation should respect the original
paper's problem formulation, methodology and
intended experimental context.

### 2. Independent Implementations

There is no mandatory universal teacher-student
architecture, distillation loss or training framework.

Each paper determines its own implementation
requirements and architectural decisions.

### 3. Reproducibility and Transparency

Implementations should clearly document their
methodology, experimental configuration, results,
limitations and deviations from the original work.

Implementing a research method, reproducing its
experiments and extending its findings are distinct
contributions and should be identified as such.
